# Mixed-behavior review

These sheets sample the whole trace. They support a provisional overall quality judgment, not precise mistake boundaries. Inspect raw frames/video when assigning a mistake interval. Success and predicate counts remain separate measurements.

Provisional quality rubric: 1 = no meaningful progress with persistent failure; 2 = attempted/partial progress with major errors; 3 = substantial coherent progress but unresolved failure or major recovery; 4 = completion with minor corrections or inefficiency; 5 = clean, effective completion with no evident errors. Record a rationale, reviewer and evidence for every assigned label; do not automatically convert success to a rating. Unclear labels remain missing.

Assistant visual ratings must be identified as such. They are not independent human annotations and do not establish the paper's metadata effect.
