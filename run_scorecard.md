## How to use the numbers

- **Continue:** mechanical checks are valid and the next experiment addresses a specific remaining question. A low success rate can still provide a useful baseline.
- **Investigate:** errors, inconsistent preprocessing, excessive clipping, large seed variation, missing labels, or intervals too wide to support a conclusion. Record the reason and next diagnostic.
- **Narrow the claim:** for example, useful usual-continuation images on LIBERO-10 without reliable subtask redirection. Keep that result rather than calling it a full π0.7 reproduction.

For final A/B uncertainty, preserve pairing by task and initial-state ID. Resample matched pairs within tasks (or report paired discordant outcomes), report per-task results, and separate variation across training seeds from variation across evaluation episodes. The two seeds per task in the runtime pilot are too few for stable per-task estimates.

Record each decision with its source artifact, sample count, settings and date. Do not tune on the final 100-episode comparison; use separate development starts for pilot debugging and settings selection.
